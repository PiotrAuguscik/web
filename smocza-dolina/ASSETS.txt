Jajo Zorzy — original model created for Smocza Dolina in Blender 4.5 LTS.
Turquoise shell, faceted jade scales and gold borders. No external textures or assets.
Editable source: delivered separately as jajo-zorzy.blend with its Python generation script.
Game meshes in egg-model.mjs are baked directly from jajo-zorzy.glb without simplifying the model.
Three geometry/material pairs are shared by all world and nest eggs.
The showroom pedestal, orbit, cameras and studio lights are excluded from the game asset.
Three.js license: vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Smok Zorzy — original model created for Smocza Dolina in Blender 4.5 LTS.
Sculpted body, curved horns, raised scales, scalloped wing membranes and a crystal tail light.
Source delivered separately as smok-zorzy.blend, smok-zorzy.glb and its generation script.
dragon-model.mjs preserves the GLB geometry, hierarchy and three exported animation clips.
The game uses the Blender wing clips with flight/glide/ground visual weights.
Luna reuses the same geometry with independent blue materials and illumination.
All textures/reflections are generated locally; no external art assets were used.
